## Findings

- `harbinger-hidden-topdeck-event` — **A private discard recovery becomes public history.** Bug · Open · Verified.
  - Where: `src/sway/engine/core.py:700` (`_answer`). Severity: P1 High.
  - Reproduction: give the acting player k09, a nonempty draw deck and a discard pile whose hidden interior contains treasure3; play k09 and recover treasure3. The other player's projected events contain a public `topdeck` event naming treasure3. The card has no reveal instruction. Keep this recovery event private to its owner, as the topdeck-hand choice already is.
  - Review backlog: `base-rules-review-corrections`.
- `library-public-set-aside` — **Public set-aside actions disappear from other players' views.** Bug · Open · Verified.
  - Where: `src/sway/engine/core.py:227` (`view_for`), `src/sway/engine/core.py:608` (`_library`), `src/sway/engine/models.py` (`OpponentView`). Severity: P2 Normal.
  - Reproduction: resolve k11 with successive action cards on the deck, decline the first and pause at the next choice. Authoritative state contains the first card in `set_aside`, but the public view has no such zone or event. The referenced rules make these set-aside cards face up. Expose this public information without exposing the still-private looked-at card, and render the zone.
  - Review backlog: `base-rules-review-corrections`.
- `snapshot-continuation-and-id-validation` — **Malformed saves pass validation but cannot safely continue.** Bug · Open · Verified.
  - Where: `src/sway/engine/serialization.py:186` (`_validate`). Severity: P2 Normal.
  - Reproductions: change a new game's `pending_effect.player` to 999; restore succeeds and the next valid decision raises `IndexError`. Reusing `next_instance_id=1` permits duplicate card IDs on a later gain. Removing both pending fields from an active game is accepted and leaves it unable to advance. Reject invalid continuations, references and identifier counters before returning a restored state, while preserving the original save.
  - Review backlog: `base-rules-review-corrections`.
- `separate-engine-branch-coverage-gate` — **The configured threshold measures combined coverage rather than branches alone.** Tooling · Open · Read.
  - Where: `scripts/coverage.sh`, `pyproject.toml` (`tool.coverage.report`). Severity: P2 Normal.
  - `--cov-branch` includes branches in the combined percentage, while `fail_under=90` applies to that combined result. High statement coverage can mask branch coverage below the accepted 90% threshold. Report and gate engine branch totals explicitly, retaining the existing combined gate.
  - Review backlog: `enforce-engine-branch-coverage`.
